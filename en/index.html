Frequently asked questions

Before you buy

Is Skynara part of Anthropic?

No. Skynara is independent and not affiliated with Anthropic. Claude is a product of Anthropic. Product names belong to their owners.

Do I need a paid Claude subscription?

Not necessarily to get started. Features and limits vary by plan and change over time: check the current details in the app.

Are the guides up to date?

AI tools change fast. The content is updated regularly and we flag when a feature may change. The Pack includes updates for 6 months.

Will I get specific results?

We do not promise any result. The guides teach you methods; results depend on your situation and your practice.

How do I receive my content?

The Guide is delivered right after payment. The Pack, Coaching and Club are sent or scheduled by email, usually within 24 hours.

Do you offer refunds?

Digital content is delivered quickly (right away for the Guide, within 24 hours for the rest), so sales are final: we do not offer refunds, except where required by law. Your consumer rights in Quebec are not affected. A problem (file not received, error in your order)? Write to us and we will fix it. The Club can be cancelled at any time to stop future payments.

What about confidential data?

Avoid pasting confidential or personal information into an AI tool without checking its privacy rules. The guides explain how to be careful.

Can AI replace a professional?

No. It can prepare, summarize and organize, but legal, tax, medical or financial advice must come from a qualified professional.
